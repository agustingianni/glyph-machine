A landscape in one letter

A single letter of text, drawn by a custom font. Three mountain ranges scroll at different speeds, the sun sets and rises, and the stars come out. The shapes, the colors, and the motion all live inside the font file.

A
time: auto
CFF2 blend moves the mountains and the sun COLRv1 variable paints darken the sky COLRv0 flat colors where COLRv1 is missing 49 masters over one loop

The font carries a TIME axis. One CSS keyframe sweeps it, and the font's charstrings blend 49 masters into each frame. Each range is a sum of sine waves that repeats every width, so the loop is seamless. Drag the slider to steer TIME yourself.
